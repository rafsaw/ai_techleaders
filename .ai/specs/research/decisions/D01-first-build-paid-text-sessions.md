# D01 — First build is paid text help; vault, MCP access and subscriptions are deferred
- Date, owner: 2026-10-15, product owner (founder A); confirmed in the om-discover session. The choice is the product owner's own, not an agent recommendation.
- Context and the options weighed: the workshop (2026-08-26) framed the MVP as "the smallest product that earns for a mentor without a call", and the board's MVP included one AI feature, an answer from the vault (`2026-09-02-miro-board-product-goals.md`). The alternative weighed was that vault-first MVP.
- Decision and why: the first build is paid text help. A mentor has a profile with links to public work (TypeScript, React or Python) and offers 25- or 50-minute text sessions. A mentee describes a blocker, picks a mentor and a session, pays upfront through Stripe, and receives written help. No video (N01), no ratings (N02). The vault, MCP access and subscriptions come in a later version. Reasons given: three of four mentees did not want a subscription or saved answers, they want an answer to a specific problem (`2026-08-20-interview-working-dev.md`, `2026-09-01-interview-backend-dev-fintech.md`, `2026-09-02-interview-platform-dev-solves-alone.md`); and the 25-hour build budget was sized for a vault scope (`2026-08-26-workshop-export.md`, `2026-09-02-miro-board-product-goals.md`).
- Statements of fact supplied in the same session: nothing beyond the research files has happened with real people; nobody has paid, booked or agreed to anything, and the product owner has not answered anyone's blocker by hand. The product owner also acts as tech lead for this project.
- Acceptance of risk: the product owner accepts building before A01 (developers will pay for a written answer from a stranger) is tested, and declines a by-hand paid test now. This is permission to take the risk, not evidence that developers will pay; A01 stays an assumption.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the vault-side claims on the board stay untested and out of the build. Revisit when D02 is reviewed (2026-12-31, stop rule D08), or sooner if A01 is refuted.
- Status: active
