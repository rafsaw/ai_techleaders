# D11 — Manual cancellation and email reporting

- Date, owner: 2026-10-17, product owner (founder A); confirmed in the interactive om-discover refresh and approved for saving after the preview.
- Origin: the PO supplied the cancellation, rescheduling and reporting rules in this session. These are product choices, not research findings; no empirical basis for the 24-hour cutoff was supplied.
- Decision: adopt R06 and R07, preserving D09 and R05.
- R06: A mentee may cancel free of charge at least 24 hours before the scheduled start, with a full refund. For cancellation less than 24 hours before the start, the fee is forfeited. Once the session starts, only a dispute may be submitted. In 1.0, the operator handles cancellation and refunds manually in Stripe (D09); self-service cancellation is deferred. Rescheduling is not offered: the mentee cancels under this rule and books a new slot.
- R07: Either participant reports no-shows and disputes by email to the operator. The operator handles settlement manually, applies R05 to no-shows, and records the outcome.
- Existing no-show settlement remains R05: mentor no-show refunded the same day; mentee no-show paid out to the mentor. Neither the reporting channel nor this decision changes those outcomes.
- Remaining uncertainty: mentor cancellation policy (Q11), the cancellation request channel and timestamp used for the cutoff (Q12), and operator contact, outcome recording, other dispute settlement and late no-show reports (Q13). No reporting deadline or exception to R05 was invented.

The PO approved these test descriptions; neither test has run, owners remain pending and dates unknown. A01's accepted untested status and D01 remain unchanged.
