- Date, owner: 2026-10-15, product owner (founder A); confirmed in the om-discover session.
- Context and the options weighed: the interviews name about twenty minutes (`2026-09-01-interview-backend-dev-fintech.md`) to the same day (`2026-09-02-interview-freelancer-react.md`); 31 of 109 Discord threads (28%) carried an urgency signal (`2026-09-04-discord-help-requests.csv`). - Decision and why: the mentee picks an available slot from the mentor's schedule and pays when booking. The 25 or 50 minutes is the session length, not waiting time, and the earliest start is not a guarantee. The product owner records this as a conscious trade-off against the twenty-minute to same-day need.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: urgent buyers may not book (A03). Revisit if the hours from payment to session start, measured after launch, show that stuck developers do not wait.
