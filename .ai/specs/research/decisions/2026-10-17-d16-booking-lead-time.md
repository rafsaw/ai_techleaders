# D16 — Booking lead time

- Date, owner: 2026-10-17, product owner (founder A); confirmed in the interactive om-discover refresh and approved for saving after preview.
- Origin: PO choice adopted from the course reference backlog as reported by the PO: full-backlog.md E01-S01, E01-S02, E01-S03, E02-S01, E03-S02, E03-S04; course authors’ decisions D07, D08 and D22. - Decision: A slot is eligible for booking only when its scheduled start is at least two hours after the moment of slot selection. The product does not promise a quick start.
- Clarifications: The two-hour minimum is measured from slot selection, not payment confirmation. It excludes the twenty-minute or within-the-hour help described in some interviews. A03 remains untested; payment-to-start waiting time is distinct from selection-to-start eligibility.
