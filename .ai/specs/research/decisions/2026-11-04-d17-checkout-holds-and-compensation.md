# D17 — Checkout holds and automatic system-failure compensation

- Date, owner: 2026-11-04, product owner; all E04 independent review findings B1–B5 and S1–S17 approved in the task instruction.
- Status: active product decision; specification design, not implemented behavior.
- Authority: [E04](../../2026-11-04-booking-and-payment.md), [product brief D17](../../product-brief.md).
- Selection uses a public slot UUID and existing validated sign-in returnTo, without a Selection entity/secret/expiry. Current D16, availability and bookability are rechecked on each relevant request, including payment claim; paid ineligible claims are compensated.
- Only Pay creates an atomic hold with a five-minute **target**, not guaranteed maximum. Overdue-held slots appear as potentially selectable in public availability; reconcile the previous provider session before review/checkout. Paid or uncertain prior payments reject the competing attempt. No release while payment or outstanding creation could still succeed; committed creation/closing markers and conditional transitions protect late responses.
- An authenticated owner can resume the existing open eligible session after cancel, or safely start a fresh attempt immediately after confirmed closure. Cancel navigation proves no financial state; never create competing holds.
- A successful payment that cannot safely book creates a durable full-refund obligation with transfer reversal and application-fee refund. Refunds can remain pending. Compensation permanently prevents that attempt from later claiming a slot; hold release is separately conditional on no other payable session/payment or unsettled creation. Refunded status alone does not justify release.
- Supersedes the earlier E04 no-hold/competing-payments design and provisional 30-minute Selection/handle design. D09/D12 manual cancellation, no-show and dispute processes remain unchanged; this automatic system-failure compensation is their narrow approved exception. D06 remains provisional 20%; D07 receipt-only remains.
- Course MVP recovery is request-triggered plus the repeat-safe operator command. No scheduler, WebSockets, event bus, additional monitoring infrastructure or new booking list. Participant details work through existing mentor booked-slot links and buyer checkout-return even without email. E05 owns lists.
- Consequence/accepted risk: holds can exceed five minutes during outage, absent traffic or unresolved creation; refunds and SMTP delivery are not instantaneous. Retain uncertain obligations for operator investigation. Keep live checkout disabled until all three E04 phases and test-mode evidence pass.
- Review by: E04 implementation review; change requires a superseding PO-approved decision. No GitHub Issues changed by this record; E04 lists deferred issue wording updates.
